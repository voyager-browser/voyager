Everything in its place

Built for the way you actually browse.

Thoughtful tools that are there when you need them and quiet when you don't.

Profiles that stay separate

Give every part of your life its own space, with independent tabs, history, bookmarks, and settings.

Tabs without the clutter

Multi-select tabs, split the view in two, or switch to a vertical rail when your tab bar gets crowded.

Privacy you can see

Block ads and trackers with built-in and custom filter lists, then tune exceptions site by site from the privacy dashboard.

Forms fill themselves

Save addresses, contacts, and payment methods, and let Voyager recall the form values you use most.

Your network, your rules

System, direct, or fixed-server proxy modes, custom user agents, and per-profile filter lists keep you in control.

Friendly to developers

Docked DevTools, developer options, keyboard shortcuts, and a Memory Saver that keeps idle tabs from weighing you down.

Privacy, made visible

Know what the browser is doing for you.

Tracker blocking is off until you turn it on. Once it's running, Voyager shows you exactly what it stopped — on the current page and across your session.

  • Per-site toggle in the page information popover
  • Busiest blocked hosts, ranked for the session
  • Allowed sites you can re-enable with one click
  • Exceptions stored per profile, not globally
